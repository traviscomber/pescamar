# Seafood Intelligence OS — Commercial Positioning

## Canonical proposition

**EN**  
Run seafood operations with fewer manual tasks and clearer decisions.

**ES**  
Opera seafood con menos tareas manuales y decisiones más claras.

## Canonical operating principle

**EN**  
Capture once → confirm the physical → automate the repetitive → escalate exceptions → decide with evidence.

**ES**  
Capturar una vez → confirmar lo físico → automatizar lo repetitivo → escalar excepciones → decidir con evidencia.

## What we sell

Seafood Intelligence OS is not positioned as a list of ERP modules, AI features or technical architecture. The customer buys a simpler operating model for seafood processing and commercial operations.

The product should explain every stage through three questions:

1. What does the system prepare or automate?
2. What must a person physically confirm or execute?
3. When does a manager need to intervene?

## Commercial story

Reception → Production → Packing → Inventory & Cold Chain → Commercial → Decision

The system carries context forward so information is captured once. People confirm physical facts and deliberate commercial decisions. Managers are brought in for exceptions rather than routine steps.

## Target operating model

- 1 generalist plant operator.
- 1 commercial / administrative owner.
- 1 manager / supervisor by exception.
- Technical administration as non-daily support.

This is a target operating model, not a staffing guarantee. Customer-specific staffing depends on real plant scale, shifts, regulation and process requirements.

## Demo order

1. Open the operating model.
2. Explain the six stages.
3. Show what the system does automatically.
4. Show what the operator confirms physically.
5. Show what reaches management as an exception.
6. Then demonstrate the supporting product surfaces: reception, production, packing, inventory, cold chain, commercial, traceability and intelligence.

Technology should appear after the operating benefit is understood.

## Technology underneath

The operating model is supported by:

- Operational Core.
- Seafood Event Graph.
- EdgeVision.
- Operational Intelligence.
- Seafood AI / Control Tower.

These are enabling layers, not the opening sales message.

## Evidence discipline

Do not promise headcount reduction, savings, yield improvement or regulatory outcomes without customer-specific evidence.

Do not present AI extraction as physical confirmation.

Do not represent missing data as zero or completed work.

Pescamar is Implementation 01 and the current operational reference. It does not define the global product boundary.

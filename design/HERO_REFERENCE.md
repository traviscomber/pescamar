# Pescamar Hero Reference — Approved Direction

**Status:** APPROVED VISUAL REFERENCE  
**Date:** 11 September 2026  
**Governed by:** `DESIGN.md`

This document does not replace `DESIGN.md`. It stores the approved visual direction for the Pescamar application hero and must be interpreted under the canonical design system.

## Approved direction

The application hero should feel like a direct digital extension of Pescamar Chile rather than an external software product.

- Real maritime photography is the primary visual layer.
- Ocean blue is dominant; white and light blue carry contrast.
- Composition keeps the operational copy on the left and the strongest photographic subject toward the right when possible.
- The hero is spacious and calm, not a dashboard wall.
- Navigation and operational controls remain visually secondary.
- One clear operational statement dominates the hero.
- The application cockpit starts immediately below the hero and carries the operational state; it must not compete with the photography.
- Avoid decorative card stacks, saturated gradients, orange accents, parallax, and marketing-heavy chrome.
- Use the canonical application typography from `DESIGN.md`: Rajdhani for system/display hierarchy and Montserrat for UI/body. The generated brand reference may show other fonts, but those are visual inspiration only and are not canonical for the app.

## Home hero target

Overline: `PESCAMAR · SEAFOOD INTELLIGENCE OS`

Primary heading: `Operación de hoy`

Context line: plant / date.

Operational message: one short statement driven by real data, for example `3 asuntos requieren atención` or the corresponding honest empty/normal state.

Primary action: one operational CTA, such as `Revisar operación`.

Optional compact footer strip: maximum 3 live facts, only when backed by current data.

## Responsive composition

- Desktop: broad photographic crop, operational copy left, visual subject right, controls top-right and discreet.
- Tablet: preserve subject and text hierarchy; controls can collapse.
- Mobile: image remains meaningful, heading max two lines, controls move out of the visual focal area, primary action remains immediately reachable.

## Design gate

Any future hero iteration should be scored against:

1. Pescamar brand recognition.
2. Operational clarity in under five seconds.
3. Real-data honesty.
4. Visual calm and hierarchy.
5. Responsive quality.
6. Compliance with `DESIGN.md` tokens, typography, accessibility and motion rules.

Target score: **9.7/10 or better** before considering the hero visually closed.
